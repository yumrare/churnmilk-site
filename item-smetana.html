Yogurt & Cultured

Smetana

The sour cream of Central and Eastern Europe, cultured cream with a clean tang and a thick, spoonable body, usually around 20 to 30 percent fat in commercial versions.

CategoryYogurt & Cultured
Maker / OriginEastern Europe
StorageKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge, with a clean spoon for serving.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the tub. Finish within days of opening.
Best usesBorscht and soups, stroganoff, dumplings, blini with caviar, and baking.
SubstitutesSour cream, or crème fraîche for richer, gentler tang.
PairingsBorscht, dumplings, blini, and beef stroganoff.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Smetana is the sour cream of Eastern European cooking, spooned over borscht and folded into sauces where Western recipes use crème fraîche. Richer versions are made by skimming the fermented top of whole milk, the farmhouse method.

Storing it

Smetana does not freeze well alone; it separates. In cooked sauces it freezes acceptably as part of the dish.

Using it

Dollop smetana over hot borscht and soups at the table. Stir it into stroganoff off the heat so the sauce stays smooth.

Swaps & pairings

American sour cream is the same role at slightly lower fat. Crème fraîche swaps in with less acidity and more stability in hot sauces.
