Butter & Spreads

Every butter in every form, salted, unsalted, cultured, whipped, and clarified, plus cream cheese and dairy spreads. Butter is churned cream, one of the oldest dairy foods, and the working fat of the kitchen.

Salted Butter

Churned cream with salt added, at about 80 percent butterfat. Salted butter is the classic table butter: spreadable, seasoned, and the longest-keeping everyday butter.

Butter & Spreads · United States

Unsalted Butter

Churned cream with no salt added: the baker's standard butter. Pure, sweet cream flavor, and full control of a recipe's seasoning.

Butter & Spreads · United States

Cultured Butter

Butter churned from fermented cream, in the French and northern European tradition. Tangy, rich, and complex, with a flavor plain butter does not have.

Butter & Spreads · France

European-Style Butter

High-butterfat butter in the European style: 82 to 85 percent fat, less water than standard butter. The pastry butter, prized for laminated doughs and rich sauces.

Butter & Spreads · Northern Europe

Whipped Butter

Butter whipped with air until light and fluffy, sold in tubs. It spreads straight from the fridge and melts fast over hot food.

Butter & Spreads · United States

Brown Butter

Butter cooked until the milk solids toast and turn nutty: the French beurre noisette. Not a product but a preparation, made in minutes from any butter.

Butter & Spreads · France

Ghee

Clarified butter from India, simmered longer than standard clarified butter so the solids toast lightly. Rich and nutty, shelf-stable, and built for high heat.

Butter & Spreads · India

Cream Cheese

A soft, fresh, mildly tangy American cheese, spreadable straight from the package. The bagel's partner, and the base of cheesecake and cream cheese frosting.
