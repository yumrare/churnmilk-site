Milk & Cream

UHT Milk

Milk pasteurized at ultra-high temperature and packed aseptically, so it keeps unopened at room temperature for months. Once opened it is ordinary fresh milk.

About it

UHT milk is heated hotter than standard pasteurization (around 275°F for a few seconds) and sealed aseptically. The high heat slightly caramelizes the milk sugars, giving a cooked, mildly sweet flavor some drinkers notice at once.

Storing it

Chill UHT milk before drinking for the best flavor. It freezes like fresh milk once opened, up to 3 months, with some separation.

Using it

Use UHT milk exactly like fresh milk in cooking and baking. Note that its heated proteins make softer curds, so cheesemakers usually avoid it.

Swaps & pairings

Fresh milk swaps in one for one. UHT is the swap when refrigeration or shopping trips are limited, not a different recipe ingredient.
