Dairy Storage Chart

How long dairy keeps, and where. Fridge and freezer life for every entry in the catalog, with how each food is stored. Figures are general guidance; a maker's own label governs its product.

ItemCategoryHow to store itShelf life
CheddarCheeseWrap in wax paper or parchment, then loosely in plastic; keep in the fridge's cheese or vegetable drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once opened; an unopened block keeps for months. Trim surface mold at least an inch deep.
MozzarellaCheeseKeep fresh mozzarella in its brine or whey in the fridge. Keep low-moisture blocks wrapped and cold.Fresh balls: a few days, best eaten quickly. Low-moisture blocks: about 2 to 3 weeks opened.
ParmesanCheeseWrap in parchment or wax paper, then foil, and refrigerate. Keep the rind on until serving.Months in the fridge when wrapped well; a wedge keeps about 2 to 3 months. Grated, about 1 week at best flavor.
BrieCheeseKeep in its original wrap or wax paper in the fridge; let it breathe rather than sealing it airtight.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge. Best eaten within a few days once ripe and cut.
GoudaCheeseWrap in wax paper or parchment and refrigerate; keep the wax rind on an uncut wedge.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut. Unopened wheels keep for months; aged Gouda keeps longest.
Swiss (Emmental)CheeseWrap in parchment or wax paper and refrigerate in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; large pieces keep longer than slices.
ProvoloneCheeseWrap tightly and refrigerate; keep away from strong-smelling foods, which it absorbs.About 2 to 3 weeks once cut; aged Provolone keeps a little longer.
FetaCheeseKeep it submerged in its brine in a sealed container in the fridge; replace with plain salted water if the brine is gone.About 2 to 3 weeks in brine once opened; it dries and sours quickly left out of liquid.
GruyèreCheeseWrap in wax paper or parchment, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese or vegetable drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut. An unopened wedge keeps for months in the fridge.
ComtéCheeseWrap in wax paper or parchment, then loosely in plastic; store in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; unopened wedges keep for months.
ManchegoCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer. A light coat of olive oil on the cut face is traditional.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; aged wedges keep longer than young ones.
Pecorino RomanoCheeseWrap tightly in foil or plastic, or in wax paper then plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 4 to 6 weeks once cut; whole wedges keep for months. It dries rather than spoils.
CamembertCheeseKeep in its box or wrapped in wax paper in the fridge; let it breathe rather than sealing it airtight.About 1 to 2 weeks. Eat within a few days of its ripest point; an ammonia smell means it is past.
GorgonzolaCheeseWrap in foil and keep in the fridge's cheese drawer, away from other foods.About 1 to 2 weeks for dolce once opened; piccante keeps about 3 to 4 weeks.
StiltonCheeseWrap in foil or wax paper and keep in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once cut. It firms and sharpens as it ages in the fridge.
RoquefortCheeseWrap in foil and store in the cheese drawer, sealed in a container to contain its aroma.About 2 to 3 weeks once opened. It softens and strengthens as it sits.
AsiagoCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks for young Asiago once cut; aged wedges keep 4 to 6 weeks.
FontinaCheeseWrap in wax paper or parchment, then loosely in plastic; store in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once cut. The cut face dries quickly, so keep it covered.
TaleggioCheeseKeep wrapped in wax paper in a lidded container in the fridge, to hold its aroma.About 1 to 2 weeks. Eat it when the center feels soft; a strong ammonia smell means it is overripe.
BurrataCheeseKeep in its liquid in the coldest part of the fridge. It is highly perishable.Best within 1 to 2 days of purchase, and by the date on the package. Do not keep opened burrata beyond a day.
HalloumiCheeseKeep in its brine in the fridge, sealed. Once opened, keep it submerged in fresh lightly salted water.Weeks unopened in brine; about 1 to 2 weeks once opened. It freezes well for up to 3 months.
PaneerCheeseKeep wrapped or in water in the fridge, sealed. Change the water daily if storing it submerged.About 3 to 5 days for fresh paneer; packaged blocks keep up to a week unopened. It freezes well for up to 3 months.
Queso FrescoCheeseKeep wrapped in the fridge, sealed in a container. If it turns slimy or sour-smelling, discard it.About 1 week once opened. It is a fresh cheese and perishes faster than aged ones.
CotijaCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; it is a hard cheese and keeps well. Surface mold can be trimmed away.
Oaxaca CheeseCheeseKeep wrapped in plastic in the fridge, sealed airtight.About 1 to 2 weeks once opened. Keep it sealed; it dries at the edges first.
HavartiCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; store in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once opened. The cut face dries first; keep it wrapped.
EdamCheeseKeep the wax on the uncut side; wrap the cut face in wax paper, then loosely in plastic.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; unopened waxed wheels keep for months.
Monterey JackCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once opened. It dries at the edges; keep it wrapped.
Pepper JackCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; store in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once opened. Keep wrapped; the pepper flecks do not change its keeping.
ColbyCheeseWrap tightly in plastic or wax paper then plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once opened. It dries faster than cheddar; keep it well wrapped.
MuensterCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; store in the cheese drawer.About 2 to 3 weeks once opened. Keep the rind on to protect the paste.
RacletteCheeseWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut. The rind protects it; keep the cut face wrapped.
LimburgerCheeseWrap in foil and seal in an airtight container in the fridge; its aroma will otherwise take over.About 2 to 3 weeks. It ripens fast; eat it when the paste gives but before it turns runny and bitter.
Smoked GoudaCheeseWrap the cut face in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; the rind slows drying on uncut faces.
JarlsbergCheeseWrap the cut face in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; keep in the cheese drawer.About 3 to 4 weeks once cut; keep the wax rind on uncut faces.
Salted ButterButter & SpreadsKeep wrapped in the fridge, away from strong-smelling foods. A covered butter dish may sit out a day or two for spreading.About 1 to 3 months in the fridge; 6 to 9 months frozen.
Unsalted ButterButter & SpreadsKeep wrapped and refrigerated, away from strong-smelling foods; it takes on flavors quickly.About 1 to 2 months in the fridge; up to 6 months frozen.
Cultured ButterButter & SpreadsKeep wrapped and refrigerated; the cultures stay happiest cold and sealed from fridge odors.About 1 to 2 months in the fridge; up to 6 months frozen.
European-Style ButterButter & SpreadsKeep wrapped and refrigerated; protect it from fridge odors like any fine butter.About 1 to 2 months in the fridge; up to 6 months frozen.
Whipped ButterButter & SpreadsKeep the tub sealed in the fridge; return it promptly after serving so it stays fluffy.About 1 to 2 months in the fridge. Do not freeze; the whipped texture breaks down.
Brown ButterButter & SpreadsPour it, solids and all, into a heatproof jar. Refrigerate or freeze in portions; re-melt gently.Use at once for the fullest aroma; refrigerated in a sealed jar it keeps about 2 weeks, frozen up to 3 months.
GheeButter & SpreadsKeep in a sealed jar away from heat and light. Always use a dry spoon; water in the jar shortens its life.About 3 months in a cool pantry; up to a year refrigerated. Keep water out of the jar.
Cream CheeseButter & SpreadsKeep refrigerated in its foil or tub, tightly rewrapped. Bring to room temperature before spreading or baking.About 2 weeks once opened; unopened foil blocks keep about a month past their date stamp in the fridge.
Clarified ButterButter & SpreadsKeep in a sealed jar in the fridge, or in a cool dark pantry for shorter periods.About 3 to 6 months in the fridge; it keeps far longer than whole butter because the solids are gone.
Compound ButterButter & SpreadsRoll in parchment or plastic into a log and refrigerate; slice off coins as needed.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge with fresh herbs; up to 3 months frozen.
Garlic & Herb ButterButter & SpreadsKeep in a sealed tub or rolled log in the fridge, wrapped tightly.About 1 week in the fridge with raw garlic; up to 3 months frozen.
Honey ButterButter & SpreadsKeep in a sealed tub in the fridge; it firms up when cold.About 2 weeks in the fridge; the honey itself is a mild preservative.
Cinnamon ButterButter & SpreadsKeep in a sealed tub in the fridge, wrapped against odors.About 2 weeks in the fridge; freezes well for up to 3 months.
Maple ButterButter & SpreadsKeep in a sealed tub in the fridge; the syrup can weep, so stir before use.About 2 weeks in the fridge; freezes well for up to 3 months.
Lemon ButterButter & SpreadsRoll into a log in parchment and refrigerate; slice coins as needed.About 1 week in the fridge; up to 3 months frozen.
Irish ButterButter & SpreadsKeep wrapped in its foil in the butter compartment; the foil blocks light and odors.About 1 month in the fridge once opened; unopened and frozen, up to 6 months.
Whey ButterButter & SpreadsKeep wrapped in foil or a sealed butter dish in the fridge.About 2 to 3 weeks in the fridge once opened; its slightly higher moisture shortens its life versus cream butter.
NeufchâtelButter & SpreadsKeep the opened brick wrapped in its foil inside a sealed container in the fridge.About 2 weeks once opened; check the date on the package. Fresh cheeses perish faster than aged ones.
Whipped Cream CheeseButter & SpreadsKeep in its tub, sealed, in the fridge. Do not leave it out beyond serving.About 2 weeks once opened; keep it sealed and use a clean knife each time.
Beurre BlancButter & SpreadsServe at once, or hold in a warm (not hot) spot or a thermos. Heat breaks the emulsion.It does not keep as a sauce; hold it warm for up to about 1 hour. Leftover sauce can be chilled and rewarmed gently once.
Whole MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its carton, not in the door. Keep the cap closed to limit odor pickup.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton. Keep it cold; it sours from the back of the fridge last.
2% MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its carton, sealed, away from the door.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
1% MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, not in the door.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Skim MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, away from light and odors.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Chocolate MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, not in the door.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton. Shake before pouring, as cocoa settles.
Strawberry MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, away from odors.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
A2 MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, not in the door.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton, like other fresh milks.
Organic MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton. The printed date applies only while unopened.Unopened ultra-pasteurized cartons keep for weeks refrigerated; once opened, use within about 7 days.
ButtermilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate in its sealed carton. Shake before use, as it separates slightly.About 2 to 3 weeks unopened; about 1 to 2 weeks after opening. It thickens and tangs further with age.
Half & HalfMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, not in the door.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton; ultra-pasteurized cartons keep longer unopened.
Heavy CreamMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, in the back of the fridge.About 2 to 3 weeks unopened; about 5 to 7 days after opening. Ultra-pasteurized keeps longest.
Whipping CreamMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, in the back of the fridge.About 2 to 3 weeks unopened; about 5 to 7 days after opening.
Light CreamMilk & CreamRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, in the back of the fridge.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Clotted CreamMilk & CreamKeep sealed in the fridge. Serve the golden crust with the cream beneath it, not skimmed off.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the jar. It is a fresh cream and perishes accordingly.
Double CreamMilk & CreamRefrigerate in its sealed carton in the back of the fridge.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Evaporated MilkMilk & CreamStore cans in a cool, dry pantry. After opening, transfer to a sealed container in the fridge; never store in the open can.Unopened cans keep about a year in the pantry. Once opened, refrigerate and use within about 5 days.
Sweetened Condensed MilkMilk & CreamStore cans in a cool, dry pantry. Transfer leftovers to a sealed jar in the fridge after opening.Unopened cans keep about a year in the pantry. Once opened, refrigerate and use within about 2 weeks.
Powdered MilkMilk & CreamKeep in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Moisture cakes and stales it.About 6 to 12 months in a cool, dry pantry for nonfat; whole-milk powder keeps about 6 months. Reconstituted, use within about 5 days refrigerated.
UHT MilkMilk & CreamStore unopened cartons in a cool pantry away from heat. Refrigerate after opening, in the carton.About 6 to 9 months unopened in the pantry. Once opened, refrigerate and use within about 7 days.
Goat MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate in its sealed carton. Goat milk turns noticeably goaty as it ages, so use it fresh.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Sheep MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate fresh milk in a sealed container. Frozen milk should thaw slowly in the fridge.Fresh sheep milk keeps about 5 days refrigerated; it is often sold frozen, keeping about 6 months frozen.
EggnogMilk & CreamRefrigerate in its sealed carton. Shake gently before pouring, as spices settle.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton. It is a fresh dairy drink despite the holiday packaging.
Malted MilkMilk & CreamKeep the powder in an airtight container in a dry pantry; it cakes with humidity.The dry mix keeps about 6 to 12 months in a cool, dry pantry. Mixed into milk, keep refrigerated and drink within a day or two.
Jersey MilkMilk & CreamRefrigerate in its bottle, sealed. Cream-top bottles should be shaken or the cream folded back in before pouring.About 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the bottle.
WheyMilk & CreamKeep in a sealed jar in the fridge. Freeze it in ice-cube trays for cooking if you make cheese often.About 5 to 7 days refrigerated in a sealed container. It sours further with age.
Greek YogurtYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Use a clean spoon each time to keep the cultures clean.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the tub. A little surface whey is normal; stir it back in.
SkyrYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its cup in the fridge. Stir any surface whey back in.About 2 to 3 weeks unopened; about 1 week after opening. Check the date on the cup.
Plain YogurtYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge, and use a clean spoon to serve.About 1 to 3 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the tub. Tang increases with age.
Fruit YogurtYogurt & CulturedKeep cups sealed and refrigerated. Stir fruit-on-the-bottom styles before eating.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the cup.
Drinkable YogurtYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed and refrigerated. Shake the bottle, as it separates lightly.About 1 to 2 weeks refrigerated; check the date on the bottle. Shake before drinking.
KefirYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its bottle in the fridge. Leave a little headspace; the cultures make gas.About 2 to 3 weeks refrigerated unopened; about 1 week after opening. It continues to ferment slowly and tangs further.
Cottage CheeseYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Some store it upside down to seal the lid with the cheese itself.About 1 to 2 weeks; check the date on the tub. It sours quickly once opened, so finish it within about 5 days.
RicottaYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. If liquid pools, it can be stirred back or drained for a firmer cheese.About 1 to 2 weeks; fresh ricotta from a deli keeps about 5 days. It sours rather than ages.
MascarponeYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. It picks up odors readily, so keep it away from strong foods.About 1 to 2 weeks; check the date on the tub. Use within a few days of opening for best quality.
QuarkYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Stir any surface whey back in.About 1 to 2 weeks; check the date on the tub. Finish within a few days of opening.
Fromage BlancYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Stir in any surface whey.About 1 to 2 weeks; check the date on the tub. Finish within a few days of opening.
LabnehYogurt & CulturedKeep in a sealed container in the fridge. Store rolled labneh balls submerged in olive oil.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; balls stored under olive oil keep up to a month. Check freshness by smell.
AyranYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed and refrigerated. Shake or whisk before serving to restore the froth.About 1 to 2 weeks refrigerated for bottled ayran; homemade ayran is best the day it is made.
LassiYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed and refrigerated. Stir or shake before serving, as it separates.Best fresh. Bottled lassi keeps about 1 week refrigerated; homemade, drink within a day.
FilmjölkYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its carton in the fridge. Stir before pouring.About 1 to 2 weeks refrigerated; it continues to culture slowly and tangs further with age.
Balkan YogurtYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Once spooned into, the gel weeps whey; pour it off or stir it in.About 1 to 3 weeks in the fridge; check the date. The set breaks once stirred, which is normal.
RyazhenkaYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its bottle or jar in the fridge. Stir before drinking.About 1 week refrigerated; homemade ryazhenka is best within about 4 days.
SmetanaYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge, with a clean spoon for serving.About 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the tub. Finish within days of opening.
Sour CreamYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Use a clean spoon each time.About 1 to 3 weeks in the fridge; check the date. A little surface liquid is normal; stir it in.
Crème FraîcheYogurt & CulturedKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Stir in any surface liquid.About 2 to 3 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the tub. It firms further as it sits.
VanillaIce Cream & FrozenKeep at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder, in the back of the freezer, lid sealed. Let it stand 5 to 10 minutes before scooping.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; it keeps safely longer but slowly loses flavor and turns icy.
ChocolateIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder, stored in the back of the freezer. Soften a few minutes before scooping.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the flavor dulls and crystals grow the longer it sits.
StrawberryIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder in the back of the freezer. Serve after a brief stand at room temperature.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; berry pieces toughen and the flavor fades over long storage.
Cookies & CreamIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder. The cookie pieces stay firmest in a consistently cold freezer.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the cookie pieces soften further the longer it stores.
Mint Chocolate ChipIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder. Mint absorbs and gives off aromas easily, so keep the lid tight.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the mint flavor fades noticeably with long storage.
Cookie DoughIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder; let the tub stand a few minutes so the dough chunks soften slightly before serving.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the dough chunks gradually firm up in deep cold.
Butter PecanIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder in the back of the freezer, and serve after a short stand.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the pecans slowly lose their crunch in storage.
Rocky RoadIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder; serve after a brief stand so the marshmallow softens.Best within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; marshmallow pieces firm up and nuts soften over time.
NeapolitanIce Cream & FrozenKeep in the back of the freezer, sealed. Press plastic wrap onto the cut face to limit ice crystals.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened. Keep it deeply frozen.
French VanillaIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, away from strong-smelling foods.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
CoffeeIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Coffee ice cream absorbs odors readily.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
PistachioIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, away from strong odors.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Salted CaramelIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. The caramel ribbon freezes harder than the base; temper before serving.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Moose TracksIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Mix-ins go stale fastest; finish the pint promptly.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened. The cups stay distinct when kept deeply frozen.
Black CherryIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, away from strong odors.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Rum RaisinIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. The alcohol lowers its freezing point slightly, so it scoops soft.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Butter BrickleIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Finish the pint promptly once opened for the candy's crunch.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened. The candy pieces stale first.
SpumoniIce Cream & FrozenKeep the mold sealed and level in the freezer. Cover cut faces with plastic wrap before refreezing.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once sliced. Refreezing cut slices dulls the layers.
SherbetIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Press plastic wrap onto the surface after serving.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 month once opened. Its low fat means ice crystals form faster.
SorbetIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, with plastic wrap on the surface.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 month once opened. It ices over faster than dairy desserts.
GelatoIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed and level in the back of the freezer. Press plastic wrap onto the surface after serving.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer; about 1 month once opened. Gelato ices over sooner than American ice cream because of its lower fat and air.
Frozen YogurtIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Press plastic wrap onto the surface after serving.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Soft ServeIce Cream & FrozenEat soft serve at the shop. At-home pints should be kept deeply frozen and sealed.Soft serve is served fresh and does not keep at home; pints labeled soft serve keep about 1 to 2 months in a home freezer before turning icy.
KulfiIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in molds or wrapped in the freezer. Slice what you need and refreeze the rest promptly.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer; about 1 month once cut. Its density protects it longer than churned desserts.
Mochi Ice CreamIce Cream & FrozenKeep sealed in its tray in the freezer, with the film on the pieces until serving.About 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 month once opened. The mochi dough dries out once exposed.
