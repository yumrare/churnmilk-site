Cheese

Taleggio

A soft Italian washed-rind cheese from Lombardy, square in shape with a rosy rind and a pale, creamy center. Its aroma is stronger than its mild, tangy flavor.

CategoryCheese
Maker / OriginLombardy, Italy
StorageKeep wrapped in wax paper in a lidded container in the fridge, to hold its aroma.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 to 2 weeks. Eat it when the center feels soft; a strong ammonia smell means it is overripe.
Best usesCheese boards, risotto, polenta, pizza, and melted onto sandwiches.
SubstitutesBrie or Camembert for a gentler washed-rind effect.
PairingsBarbera, crusty bread, mostarda, and dried fruit.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Taleggio has been made in the Val Taleggio since at least the 10th century and is protected today. Squares are washed in brine as they age about 6 to 10 weeks, building the colored rind and a paste that bulges softly when ripe.

Storing it

Store away from other cheeses. Do not freeze; the soft paste turns watery and grainy.

Using it

Bring Taleggio to room temperature so the center turns supple. The rind is edible but assertive; many trim it before melting the cheese into risotto.

Swaps & pairings

Brie and Camembert cover the soft, creamy role with bloomy rather than washed rinds. A ripe Époisses-style cheese is closer but far stronger.
