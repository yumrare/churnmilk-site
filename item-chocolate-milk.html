Milk & Cream

Chocolate Milk

Milk flavored with cocoa and sweetener, usually made from whole or 2% milk. It ranges from lightly chocolaty drinking milk to thick, dessert-like versions.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, not in the door.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton. Shake before pouring, as cocoa settles.
Best usesDrinking, post-exercise recovery, lunchboxes, and milkshakes.
SubstitutesPlain milk plus chocolate syrup, stirred in the glass.
PairingsPeanut butter sandwiches, cookies, and post-workout snacks.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Chocolate milk is often made from partly skimmed milk with cocoa and sweetener, and large dairies recover the cocoa that settles by agitating the carton before pouring. Its mix of fluid, sugar, and protein is why sports studies keep testing it as a recovery drink.

Storing it

Keep sealed; the added sugar does not extend its life. Chocolate milk freezes acceptably for up to 2 months, with some separation on thawing.

Using it

Serve chocolate milk very cold and shake the carton first. It also makes a quick milkshake base blended with ice cream or frozen banana.

Swaps & pairings

Milk with chocolate syrup is the same drink mixed at home. For cooking, plain milk and cocoa powder keep the sugar under your control.
