Milk & Cream

Whey

The liquid left after milk curdles and the curds are removed in cheesemaking. It is thin, pale yellow-green, mildly sweet or tangy depending on the cheese it came from.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep in a sealed jar in the fridge. Freeze it in ice-cube trays for cooking if you make cheese often.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days refrigerated in a sealed container. It sours further with age.
Best usesBread and pizza doughs, smoothies, soups, ricotta making, and feeding sourdough starters.
SubstitutesWater or milk in baking (with different results), buttermilk for tang in marinades.
PairingsSmoothies, bread doughs, soups, and marinades.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Whey is about 93 percent water with lactose, minerals, and the whey proteins that heat precipitates as ricotta. Sweet whey from rennet cheeses and acid whey from yogurt straining differ; the strained-yogurt kind is tangier and harder to use.

Storing it

Whey freezes well for up to 6 months. Its protein makes it a poor candidate for long counter storage; keep it cold from the start.

Using it

Swap whey for the water in bread and pizza dough for a softer crumb and better browning. Add it to smoothies for protein and mild tang.

Swaps & pairings

In bread doughs, water swaps in but loses the softening sugars. Buttermilk gives more tang where whey is used as a marinade.
