Yogurt & Cultured

Ayran

A Turkish savory yogurt drink of yogurt, water, and salt, whisked to a light froth. It is thin, refreshing, and unsweetened, served cold with grilled food.

CategoryYogurt & Cultured
Maker / OriginTurkey
StorageKeep sealed and refrigerated. Shake or whisk before serving to restore the froth.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 to 2 weeks refrigerated for bottled ayran; homemade ayran is best the day it is made.
Best usesDrinking with kebabs and grilled meats, cooling spicy food, and summer refreshment.
SubstitutesSalted kefir, or thinned plain yogurt with salt.
PairingsKebabs, grilled meats, börek, and spicy dishes.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Ayran is Turkey's national drink by declaration and custom: churned from yogurt with the butter removed, then re-thinned with water and salt. The froth matters; vendors whisk or pour it from a height to raise foam before serving.

Storing it

Ayran separates as it sits; a quick whisk fixes it. Do not freeze; the texture turns watery.

Using it

Whisk yogurt, cold water, and salt until frothy and serve at once over no ice, the traditional way, with grilled meats and pastries.

Swaps & pairings

Plain yogurt whisked with water and salt is ayran itself. Kefir with a pinch of salt gives a tangier, thinner version.
