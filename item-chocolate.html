Ice Cream & Frozen

Chocolate

Ice cream flavored with cocoa or chocolate: one of the two oldest ice cream flavors and a permanent fixture in the top three sellers.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder, stored in the back of the freezer. Soften a few minutes before scooping.
Shelf lifeBest within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the flavor dulls and crystals grow the longer it sits.
Best usesSundaes, shakes, ice cream cakes, and the chocolate third of Neapolitan.
SubstitutesChocolate gelato, double chocolate, or chocolate frozen yogurt.
PairingsPeanut butter sauce, raspberries, salted caramel, and coffee.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Chocolate ice cream is made by blending cocoa powder or melted chocolate into the base before churning. Frozen chocolate drinks were enjoyed in Europe in the 1600s and 1700s, and chocolate was one of the earliest ice cream flavors.

Storing it

A grayish bloom or icy top layer signals temperature swings, not spoilage; scrape it off or stir it in. Keep the lid tight, since chocolate ice cream readily absorbs freezer odors.

Using it

Chocolate is the backbone flavor of sundaes and ice cream cakes, and it pairs with nearly every topping. In Neapolitan, it sits between vanilla and strawberry; in shakes, it takes to malt powder and espresso.

Swaps & pairings

Double chocolate adds chips or fudge for more intensity. Chocolate gelato is denser and served softer. Chocolate frozen yogurt lightens the profile with tang. All three swap one for one in sundaes and shakes.
