Ice Cream & Frozen

Neapolitan

A tri-flavor ice cream sold in one carton, classically chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry in three distinct bands. It is a sliceable, ready-for-anyone dessert.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep in the back of the freezer, sealed. Press plastic wrap onto the cut face to limit ice crystals.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened. Keep it deeply frozen.
Best usesBanana splits, ice cream sandwiches, sundaes, and children's birthday servings.
SubstitutesBuying the three flavors separately, or a homemade layered terrine.
PairingsBanana splits, wafer rolls, and hot fudge sauce.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Neapolitan's three stripes reflect an immigrant's compromise: travelers who could not agree on a flavor got all three in one carton. The classic order is chocolate, vanilla, strawberry, though modern cartons vary the combination.

Storing it

Neapolitan melts unevenly because each flavor has different solids; refreezing melted sections is discouraged. Serve firm from the freezer.

Using it

Slice Neapolitan from the carton into even bands for sandwiches and cakes. Scoop across the colors for a striped serving in a sundae glass.

Swaps & pairings

The three classics bought individually give the same scoop with better texture. Layered homemade terrines swap in for celebrations.
