Yogurt & Cultured

Fromage Blanc

A fresh French cheese of curdled milk drained to a soft, spoonable consistency, similar to quark. It is mild, fresh, and slightly tart, often sold in tubs like yogurt.

CategoryYogurt & Cultured
Maker / OriginFrance
StorageKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Stir in any surface whey.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 to 2 weeks; check the date on the tub. Finish within a few days of opening.
Best usesDesserts with fruit, cheesecakes, dips, spreads, and eating with honey.
SubstitutesQuark, or Greek yogurt for a tangier spoonful.
PairingsBerries, honey, herbs, and fruit coulis.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Fromage blanc is eaten in France like yogurt, often with sugar, fruit, or honey, and it is the base of many fresh-cheese desserts. It is drained only lightly, so it stays spoonable and glossy rather than sliceable.

Storing it

Fromage blanc does not freeze well; it turns watery. Keep it cold and use it fresh.

Using it

Serve fromage blanc with berries and honey, fold it into light cheesecakes, or whisk it with herbs for a fresh dip.

Swaps & pairings

Quark is nearly interchangeable, a little firmer. Greek yogurt gives more tang and less sweetness of flavor.
