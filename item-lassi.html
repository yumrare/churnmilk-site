Yogurt & Cultured

Lassi

An Indian yogurt drink blended with water or milk, made sweet, often with mango or rose, or salted and spiced. It is frothy, cooling, and served with meals.

CategoryYogurt & Cultured
Maker / OriginIndia
StorageKeep sealed and refrigerated. Stir or shake before serving, as it separates.
Shelf lifeBest fresh. Bottled lassi keeps about 1 week refrigerated; homemade, drink within a day.
Best usesDrinking with spicy meals, mango desserts, and cooling summer drinks.
SubstitutesDrinkable yogurt for the sweet version; ayran for the salted one.
PairingsCurries, biryani, samosas, and mango desserts.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Lassi is older than the blender by centuries, churned in a clay pot with a wooden whisk. The sweet version is a dessert drink; the salted version, chaas in some regions, is thinner and spiced, closer to ayran with cumin.

Storing it

Lassi does not freeze well. Make it in the quantity you will drink; the yogurt base keeps longer than the finished drink.

Using it

Blend yogurt with water or milk, ice, and either sugar and fruit or salt and spice, until frothy. Serve immediately, very cold.

Swaps & pairings

Bottled drinkable yogurt approximates sweet lassi. Salted lassi swaps closely with ayran plus roasted cumin.
