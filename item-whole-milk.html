Milk & Cream

Whole Milk

Fluid milk at its full fat level, about 3.25 percent butterfat. It is the standard milk of the dairy case, sold fresh and refrigerated.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its carton, not in the door. Keep the cap closed to limit odor pickup.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton. Keep it cold; it sours from the back of the fridge last.
Best usesDrinking, cereal, baking, sauces, soups, and coffee.
Substitutes2% milk for drinking; half & half plus water for cooking in a pinch.
PairingsCereal, coffee, cookies, and oatmeal.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

In the United States, whole milk is standardized at 3.25 percent milkfat. It separates less and tastes fuller than reduced-fat milks because the fat carries flavor and gives body to cooking, which is why it is the default for baking and sauces.

Storing it

Milk absorbs odors, so keep it sealed and away from strong foods. It freezes for up to 3 months; thaw in the fridge and shake, as it may separate slightly.

Using it

Use whole milk as the default for baking, béchamel, and chowders. Bring it to room temperature before baking for more even mixing.

Swaps & pairings

2% milk drinks nearly the same with less richness. For sauces and baking, a splash of cream in 2% milk restores the body.
