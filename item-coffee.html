Ice Cream & Frozen

Coffee

An ice cream flavored with coffee, ranging from lightly tan and sweet to dark and intense. It is a classic American parlor flavor and a base for coffee shakes.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Coffee ice cream absorbs odors readily.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Best usesAffogato, frappes and milkshakes, tiramisu bases, and eating plain.
SubstitutesEspresso or java chip styles for more intensity, or coffee yogurt frozen briefly.
PairingsChocolate, espresso shots (affogato), tiramisu, and biscotti.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Coffee ice cream's trick is balance: too little coffee reads as sweet cream, too much turns bitter when frozen, since cold mutes sweetness and sharpens bitterness. Good versions brew strong coffee or steep grounds in the cream base itself.

Storing it

Seal tightly; its coffee note fades with freezer age. Temper a few minutes before scooping.

Using it

Pour a shot of hot espresso over a scoop for an affogato. Blend coffee ice cream with milk and ice for a frappe; add chocolate for a mocha shake.

Swaps & pairings

Affogato over vanilla ice cream is the drinkable cousin. Espresso-flavored varieties swap in with more punch.
