Ice Cream & Frozen

Pistachio

An ice cream flavored with pistachio paste and dotted with nut pieces. Its flavor is rich, buttery, and unmistakably nutty, with a pale natural green.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginSouthern Italy
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, away from strong odors.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Best usesSundaes, cannoli fillings, layered cakes, and eating plain.
SubstitutesAlmond ice cream for a similar nut flavor, or pistachio gelato for a denser scoop.
PairingsDark chocolate, rose water, honey, and olive oil (Sicilian style).
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Pistachio is an old gelato and ice cream flavor with a notoriously fickle color: the nut itself is a pale green-brown, so the vivid green of many pints comes from coloring. Premium versions are a dusty sage and taste distinctly of the nut, not almond.

Storing it

Nut oils oxidize, so finish the pint within a month of opening for the best flavor. Temper before scooping.

Using it

Serve pistachio as the star of a simple sundae, or pair it with chocolate and rose notes. Buy the palest version you can find; it usually means real nuts.

Swaps & pairings

Almond ice cream is the nearest common swap. Pistachio gelato, where available, is the same flavor at lower fat and warmer serving temperature.
