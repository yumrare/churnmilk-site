Butter & Spreads

Clarified Butter

Butter with its water and milk solids removed, leaving clear golden butterfat. It has a higher smoke point than whole butter and a clean, concentrated flavor.

CategoryButter & Spreads
Maker / OriginFrance
StorageKeep in a sealed jar in the fridge, or in a cool dark pantry for shorter periods.
Shelf lifeAbout 3 to 6 months in the fridge; it keeps far longer than whole butter because the solids are gone.
Best usesSearing, sautéing, hollandaise and béarnaise, dipping shellfish, and roasting.
SubstitutesGhee, or a neutral oil plus a little whole butter for flavor.
PairingsLobster, crab, asparagus, and hollandaise.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Clarifying removes the water and milk solids from butter, leaving pure butterfat. What remains tolerates higher heat without burning and keeps far longer. It is the cooking medium behind many classic sauces and sautés.

Storing it

Skim any foam and strain through cheesecloth when making it; stray solids shorten its life. It solidifies when cold.

Using it

Use clarified butter where whole butter would burn: hot pans, roasts, and clarified dipping butter for lobster and crab. Warm it gently until liquid.

Swaps & pairings

Ghee is the closest match, nuttier from longer cooking. For sautéing, use oil for heat and finish with butter for flavor.
