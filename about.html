About ChurnMilk

ChurnMilk is a reference guide to dairy: cheeses, butters, spreads, milks, yogurts, and frozen dairy, each cataloged with its origin, storage, shelf life, uses, substitutes, and pairings.

Entries are compiled from verified sources only, and each one shows the date it was last checked. Figures such as shelf life are general guidance; a maker's own label governs its product.

If an entry has an error, send the correction through Contact Us. Corrections are verified against a source, the entry is updated, and its last-checked date is refreshed.
