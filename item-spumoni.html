Ice Cream & Frozen

Spumoni

A molded Italian-style dessert of layered ice creams, classically chocolate, cherry, and pistachio, with candied fruit and nuts. It is served in slices, not scoops.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginSouthern Italy
StorageKeep the mold sealed and level in the freezer. Cover cut faces with plastic wrap before refreezing.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once sliced. Refreezing cut slices dulls the layers.
Best usesSliced desserts, holiday tables, and Italian-American dinners.
SubstitutesA Neapolitan terrine, or layered gelato from a gelateria.
PairingsBiscotti, espresso, amaretti, and amaretto.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Spumoni layers reflect its Neapolitan origins: chocolate, cherry, and pistachio, with candied fruit and nuts folded in. It is served sliced from a mold, which is why it is the celebratory ice cream of Italian-American restaurants.

Storing it

Slice spumoni straight from the freezer with a hot, dry knife, wiping between cuts for clean bands. Thaw slices a few minutes before serving.

Using it

Slice spumoni into wedges or bricks from the mold and serve with biscotti and espresso. Let slices stand a few minutes so the layers soften evenly.

Swaps & pairings

Neapolitan is the everyday layered substitute. Layered flavors from a gelato shop give the same festive slice.
