Milk & Cream

Whipping Cream

Cream with about 30 to 36 percent butterfat, formulated to whip. It produces softer, lighter peaks than heavy cream and pours more easily.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, in the back of the fridge.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 weeks unopened; about 5 to 7 days after opening.
Best usesWhipped toppings, mousses, trifles, cream sauces, and coffee.
SubstitutesHeavy cream, which whips firmer and keeps peaks longer.
PairingsFruit desserts, hot chocolate, coffee, and trifles.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The line between whipping cream and heavy cream is a few points of fat, but it shows in the bowl: whipping cream makes soft, billowy peaks that slump sooner. It is the better choice for folding into mousses and spooning over desserts.

Storing it

Freeze leftover whipping cream for cooking up to 2 months; it will not whip after thawing. Whipped dollops freeze well on a tray.

Using it

Whip whipping cream cold for soft peaks to fold into mousses and spoon over fruit. Sweeten at the end of whipping, not the start.

Swaps & pairings

Heavy cream is the direct swap with a more stable result. Half & half cannot be whipped and is not a substitute here.
