Milk & Cream

Eggnog

A seasonal dairy drink of milk, cream, egg yolk, sugar, and spices such as nutmeg and cinnamon. It is sold fresh in cartons during the winter holidays.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate in its sealed carton. Shake gently before pouring, as spices settle.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton. It is a fresh dairy drink despite the holiday packaging.
Best usesDrinking at the holidays, lattes, French toast batter, and baking.
SubstitutesMilk and cream with egg yolk, sugar, and nutmeg whisked in (the homemade original).
PairingsNutmeg, coffee, rum or bourbon (for adults), and gingerbread.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Eggnog began as a spiced milk-and-ale drink in England and became an American holiday standard, spiked or not. Commercial eggnog is a formulated dairy drink: milk, cream, egg yolks, sugar, and spices, sometimes with thickeners for body.

Storing it

Eggnog freezes for up to 2 months, though it may separate; blend briefly after thawing. Do not keep opened eggnog past a week.

Using it

Serve eggnog cold with fresh nutmeg grated over it. Use leftover eggnog in French toast, pancakes, and quick breads in place of milk.

Swaps & pairings

A homemade batch is the only true swap: milk, cream, yolks, sugar, and spice. Vanilla milk with nutmeg is a light, egg-free approximation.
