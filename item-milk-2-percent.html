Milk & Cream

2% Milk

Milk with the fat reduced to 2 percent. It pours thinner than whole milk but keeps much of its body, and it is the most widely sold milk in the United States.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its carton, sealed, away from the door.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Best usesDrinking, cereal, coffee, baking, and everyday cooking.
SubstitutesWhole milk for richness, or 1% milk for a lighter glass.
PairingsCereal, coffee, tea, and sandwiches.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The percentages on milk labels refer to fat by weight, not calories: 2% milk still gets about a fifth of its calories from fat. It was introduced as a lighter everyday milk and became the most common choice in American refrigerators.

Storing it

Keep the carton closed to limit odor pickup. It freezes for up to 3 months; thaw in the fridge and shake before using.

Using it

Use 2% milk for everyday drinking, cereal, and most baking. In rich sauces and custards, whole milk gives a fuller result.

Swaps & pairings

Whole milk swaps in with more body. Between 2% and 1%, the difference shows most in cooking; drinking is closer.
