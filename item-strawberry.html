Ice Cream & Frozen

Strawberry

Sweet cream ice cream flavored with real strawberries or strawberry puree. One of the Neapolitan trio and a summertime staple.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder in the back of the freezer. Serve after a brief stand at room temperature.
Shelf lifeBest within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; berry pieces toughen and the flavor fades over long storage.
Best usesNeapolitan, shortcake, sundaes, shakes, and banana splits.
SubstitutesStrawberry frozen yogurt, strawberry gelato, or raspberry ice cream.
PairingsBalsamic glaze, basil, shortcake, and dark chocolate.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Strawberry ice cream gets its flavor and pink color from strawberries, fresh, frozen, or as puree, folded into a sweet cream base. Quality varies honestly with the fruit: versions with real berry pieces taste markedly fresher.

Storing it

Strawberry ice cream shows freezer burn early as pale, icy patches on the pink surface. Press parchment onto the surface after scooping and keep the lid tight to protect the fruit.

Using it

Strawberry is the bright third of Neapolitan and the classic over warm shortcake. Its acidity cuts rich toppings, and in a banana split it balances the chocolate and caramel sauces beside it.

Swaps & pairings

Strawberry frozen yogurt swaps in lighter and tangier. Strawberry gelato is denser and fruit-forward. Raspberry ice cream keeps the berry role with a sharper edge. All work in the Neapolitan lineup and on shortcake.
