Ice Cream & Frozen

Black Cherry

An almond-scented or vanilla base studded with dark, tart black cherries. It is pinkish-purple from the fruit, with a sweet-tart contrast.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, away from strong odors.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Best usesSundaes, banana splits, milkshakes, and eating plain.
SubstitutesCherry vanilla ice cream, or cherry sorbet for a dairy-free fruit version.
PairingsChocolate, almonds, whipped cream, and waffle cones.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Black cherry ice cream came from the soda-fountain era's love of cherry sodas and sundaes. The dark, almost black color comes from the cherry variety and often a little coloring. Tart cherry pieces cut the sweet base, which is why it works better than sweet cherries would.

Storing it

The fruit pieces freeze hardest; temper a few minutes before scooping. Finish within about a month of opening.

Using it

Serve black cherry in a waffle cone or bowl as a sundae with whipped cream. Pair it with chocolate scoops for a cherry-chocolate combination.

Swaps & pairings

Cherry vanilla is the closest flavor, lighter in fruit. A cherry sorbet plus whipped cream approximates the bowl without dairy.
