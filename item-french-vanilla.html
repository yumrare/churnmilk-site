Ice Cream & Frozen

French Vanilla

A premium vanilla ice cream made from a cooked egg-yolk custard base, rather than plain sweet cream. It is richer, denser, and yellower than standard vanilla.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginFrance
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer, away from strong-smelling foods.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Best usesÀ la mode desserts, sundaes, floats, and eating plain.
SubstitutesPremium vanilla ice cream, ideally labeled with eggs in the ingredients.
PairingsWarm apple pie, peach cobbler, chocolate cake, and espresso.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The name signals eggs: French-style custard ice cream uses an egg-yolk custard base rather than the simpler Philadelphia style of the American standard. The result is dense, silky, and pale gold, and it scoops firmer than regular vanilla.

Storing it

Custard-style ice cream keeps its texture well frozen. Let it sit at room temperature a few minutes before scooping.

Using it

Serve French vanilla à la mode with warm pies and cobblers, where its custard density stands up to the heat. Let it temper before scooping.

Swaps & pairings

A dense premium vanilla is the direct swap. For cooking, homemade custard-style bases are richer still.
