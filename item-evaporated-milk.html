Milk & Cream

Evaporated Milk

Unsweetened milk concentrated by evaporating about 60 percent of its water, then canned and sterilized. It pours like light cream and tastes faintly cooked.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageStore cans in a cool, dry pantry. After opening, transfer to a sealed container in the fridge; never store in the open can.
Shelf lifeUnopened cans keep about a year in the pantry. Once opened, refrigerate and use within about 5 days.
Best usesPumpkin pie, tres leches, chowders, mac and cheese, fudge, and coffee.
SubstitutesHalf & half or light cream for sauces; whole milk reduced by simmering for baking.
PairingsPumpkin pie, tres leches cake, coffee, and mac and cheese.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Evaporated milk was a frontier and wartime staple because it kept without refrigeration, and it remains standard in pumpkin pie and many Latin American and Southeast Asian desserts. It is not interchangeable with sweetened condensed milk.

Storing it

Shake the can before opening. Leftover evaporated milk freezes for up to 2 months; whisk after thawing.

Using it

Use evaporated milk straight from the can in pie fillings and chowders. Diluted with an equal part of water, it approximates whole milk.

Swaps & pairings

Half & half covers soups and sauces. For pie fillings, simmer whole milk down by about half to concentrate it the same way.
