Whipped Cream Cheese

Cream cheese whipped with air (and sometimes a little milk) into a light, fluffy spread. It spreads straight from the fridge without tearing bread or bagels.

CategoryButter & Spreads
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep in its tub, sealed, in the fridge. Do not leave it out beyond serving.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 weeks once opened; keep it sealed and use a clean knife each time.
Best usesBagels and toast, sandwich spreads, dips, and frostings for a lighter texture.
SubstitutesBrick cream cheese, softened and whipped with a spoonful of milk.
PairingsBagels, toast, smoked salmon, and vegetable sticks.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Whipping air into cream cheese changes how it spreads more than how it tastes: a cup of whipped weighs less than a cup of brick, so per-spoon calories drop even though the recipe is the same. Tubs often add small amounts of stabilizer to hold the air.

Storing it

Whipped cream cheese does not freeze well; the foam collapses and weeps. Buy the tub size you will finish.

Using it

Spread whipped cream cheese as is, or fold in herbs, scallions, or smoked salmon for flavored spreads. It is not ideal for baking, where brick cheese measures better.

Swaps & pairings

Whip softened brick cream cheese with a teaspoon of milk to make your own. Neufchâtel gives a lighter, tangier spread.
