Ice Cream & Frozen

Kulfi

An Indian frozen dessert of milk reduced by slow simmering until thick, sweetened and flavored (cardamom, saffron, pistachio), then frozen dense in molds.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginIndia
StorageKeep sealed in molds or wrapped in the freezer. Slice what you need and refreeze the rest promptly.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer; about 1 month once cut. Its density protects it longer than churned desserts.
Best usesSliced desserts, festival sweets, and falooda.
SubstitutesDense vanilla ice cream for the creamy role, or condensed-milk ice cream bases at home.
PairingsCardamom, saffron, pistachios, and rose syrup.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Kulfi predates churned ice cream in India, made by reducing milk for hours until it thickens, then freezing it unstirred in molds. It sets dense, almost chewy, with a caramelized milk flavor no churned ice cream has. Traditionally it is sliced from the mold onto a leaf.

Storing it

Kulfi keeps its texture better than churned ice cream when frozen. Slice with a warm knife and serve at once.

Using it

Unmold kulfi and slice it into rounds for falooda or plain service. Top with pistachios and a drizzle of rose syrup.

Swaps & pairings

A dense premium vanilla is the nearest grocery swap without the caramelized note. Homemade condensed-milk ice cream approximates the density.
