Creameries

Every maker and origin in the catalog, with the entries that come from it.

Cheddar, Somerset, England

The county in southwest England where Cheddar was born. Its pastures have been cheese country for centuries, and the village of Cheddar still lends its name to the world's most popular cheese style.

1 entry

Campania, Italy

The region of Naples in southern Italy, home of mozzarella. Buffalo have grazed its plains for centuries, and its stretched-curd cheeses remain the benchmark for pasta filata.

1 entry

Emilia-Romagna, Italy

Northern Italy's food heartland, the home of Parmigiano-Reggiano. Its plains produce the milk, its aging rooms hold wheels for years, and its name is protected by law.

1 entry

Île-de-France, France

The region around Paris, and the birthplace of Brie. Its soft, bloomy-rind cheeses have been made here for centuries and are named for the historic Brie district within it.

1 entry

Netherlands

The Netherlands has made and traded cheese for over a thousand years. Gouda and Edam anchor its tradition, and Dutch cheese markets still weigh and grade wheels by hand.

1 entry

Emme Valley, Switzerland

A river valley in the canton of Bern, home of Emmental. Its alpine milk and large copper vats produce some of the biggest cheese wheels in the world.

1 entry

Southern Italy

Italy's south is pasta filata country: Campania, Puglia, and Basilicata stretch their curds into mozzarella and Provolone, a technique the region has practiced for centuries.

1 entry

Greece

Greek dairying is built on sheep and goats suited to its rocky hills. Feta, matured in brine, is its signature cheese and a protected name under European law.

1 entry

France

France brings the technique: cultured butter from fermented cream, and brown butter, the beurre noisette of its kitchens. French dairy craft favors fermentation and gentle cooking.

2 entries

Northern Europe

The butter heartland of Europe, from France to Denmark and Ireland, where higher-butterfat butter is the standard and pastry is built on it.

1 entry

India

India is the home of ghee, clarified butter cooked a step further, central to its cooking for thousands of years and valued for keeping without refrigeration.

1 entry

Torquay, England

A seaside town in Devon, England, where a culinary student's 1973 competition entry, mint chocolate chip, became one of the world's favorite ice cream flavors.

1 entry

United States

America built its dairy case on standards: graded butter, Philadelphia-style cream cheese, and the ice cream parlor flavors that spread worldwide in the twentieth century.
