Butter & Spreads

Maple Butter

A compound butter of butter whipped with maple syrup. It is pale, sweet, and faintly woody, a New England and Canadian staple for pancakes and cornbread.

CategoryButter & Spreads
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep in a sealed tub in the fridge; the syrup can weep, so stir before use.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 weeks in the fridge; freezes well for up to 3 months.
Best usesPancakes, waffles, French toast, cornbread, and roasted vegetables.
SubstitutesHoney butter, or plain butter with warm maple syrup poured over.
PairingsPancakes, waffles, cornbread, and roasted squash.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

True maple butter, or maple cream, contains no dairy at all; it is maple syrup cooked and stirred into a spread. The dairy version here, butter whipped with maple syrup, is a compound butter used on pancakes and cornbread in maple country.

Storing it

Use real maple syrup, not flavored syrup, for both flavor and keeping quality. Serve soft, at cool room temperature.

Using it

Whip softened butter with maple syrup until combined and spread on hot pancakes and cornbread. A pinch of salt sharpens the maple flavor.

Swaps & pairings

Honey butter is the closest sweet swap. Plain butter under warm maple syrup on the same pancake is the unmixed original.
