Netherlands

The Netherlands has made and traded cheese for over a thousand years. Gouda and Edam anchor its tradition, and Dutch cheese markets still weigh and grade wheels by hand.

Gouda

A Dutch cow's-milk cheese named for the city of Gouda. It ranges from young, mild, and buttery to aged wheels with caramel notes and crunchy crystals.

Cheese · Netherlands

Edam

A semi-hard Dutch cheese named for the town of Edam, usually sold coated in red wax. It is mild, slightly salty, and nutty, made from partly skimmed milk.

Cheese · Netherlands

Smoked Gouda

A Dutch-style cheese made by smoking young Gouda over hickory or similar wood. It has a brown rind, a pale supple paste, and a pronounced smoky, caramel flavor.

Cheese · Netherlands
