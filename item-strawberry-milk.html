Milk & Cream

Strawberry Milk

Milk flavored with strawberry syrup and sweetener, sold ready to drink, usually pink. It is typically made from whole or 2% milk.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, away from odors.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Best usesDrinking, lunchboxes, milkshakes, and pink milk steamers for children.
SubstitutesPlain milk with strawberry syrup, or strawberry yogurt drinks for a tangier glass.
PairingsVanilla wafers, shortbread, and peanut butter sandwiches.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Strawberry milk belongs to the flavored-milk family that includes banana and vanilla milks around the world. The flavor is carried in a syrup blended into the milk before pasteurization, so it is uniform rather than layered like a homemade version.

Storing it

Keep the carton closed; shake gently before pouring. It freezes for up to 2 months with some separation on thawing.

Using it

Serve strawberry milk ice cold. Blend it with vanilla ice cream for a strawberry shake, or steam it gently for a warm drink.

Swaps & pairings

Milk plus strawberry syrup is the exact homemade formula. A strawberry kefir or yogurt drink swaps in with more tang and body.
