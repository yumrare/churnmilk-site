Butter & Spreads

Whey Butter

Butter churned from the cream that rises on whey left over from cheesemaking. It is slightly tangy, a little lower in fat than cream butter, and traditional in Britain.

CategoryButter & Spreads
Maker / OriginNorthern Europe
StorageKeep wrapped in foil or a sealed butter dish in the fridge.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 weeks in the fridge once opened; its slightly higher moisture shortens its life versus cream butter.
Best usesBaking, spreading on crumpets and scones, and finishing vegetables.
SubstitutesCultured butter for the tang, or ordinary salted butter.
PairingsCrumpets, scones, oatcakes, and farmhouse bread.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Whey butter nearly vanished with factory cheesemaking, which sends whey away as a byproduct, but farmhouse makers kept it alive. Because whey holds little fat, the butter is made in small batches and prized by bakers who know it.

Storing it

Keep it away from strong odors, as all butter absorbs them. Whey butter freezes acceptably for up to 3 months.

Using it

Treat whey butter like cultured butter: spread it where its tang shows, on crumpets and scones, and bake with it when you want a deeper flavor.

Swaps & pairings

Cultured butter gives the same fermented note from cream. Standard salted butter covers baking and spreading without the tang.
