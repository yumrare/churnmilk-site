Butter Pecan

Vanilla or brown-sugar ice cream filled with butter-roasted pecans. A Southern American classic with a deep, toasted-nut flavor.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder in the back of the freezer, and serve after a short stand.
Shelf lifeBest within 1 to 2 months at 0 degrees Fahrenheit; the pecans slowly lose their crunch in storage.
Best usesSundaes, pie à la mode, and Southern dessert menus.
SubstitutesPralines and cream, butter walnut, or vanilla with candied pecans on top.
PairingsBourbon caramel, warm pecan pie, and maple syrup.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Butter pecan comes from the American South, where pecans are native and abundant. Roasting the nuts in butter before adding them is the defining step; it deepens their flavor and keeps them from going soft in the frozen base.

Storing it

Nuts absorb moisture in the freezer, so butter pecan is at its best young. If the pecans have gone soft or the base tastes stale or rancid, the tub has kept too long.

Using it

Butter pecan is the grown-up scoop of the freezer case: less sweet, nutty, and rich. It belongs beside warm pecan pie and under bourbon caramel, and it makes a quietly luxurious milkshake.

Swaps & pairings

Pralines and cream is the closest swap, sweeter, with caramelized pecans and a caramel swirl. Butter walnut trades the nut. Vanilla with fresh candied pecans on top restores the crunch that storage takes away.
