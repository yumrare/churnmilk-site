Butter & Spreads

Garlic & Herb Butter

A compound butter of butter, garlic, and usually parsley and salt. It is the spread for garlic bread and the finishing butter for snails, steak, and shrimp.

CategoryButter & Spreads
Maker / OriginFrance
StorageKeep in a sealed tub or rolled log in the fridge, wrapped tightly.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 week in the fridge with raw garlic; up to 3 months frozen.
Best usesGarlic bread, escargots, shrimp scampi, steaks, and vegetables.
SubstitutesGarlic-infused olive oil for spreading and cooking.
PairingsBaguette, shrimp, steak, mushrooms, and green beans.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Garlic butter is the best-known compound butter. Garlic confit or roasted garlic makes it sweeter and mellower; raw garlic makes it sharp. Either way it is the backbone of garlic bread and escargots bourguignonne, the classic snail dish.

Storing it

Raw garlic in butter should be refrigerated and used promptly; freezing is the safe way to keep it longer.

Using it

Spread garlic butter thick on halved baguettes and toast until crisp at the edges. Melt it over shrimp, steak, and mushrooms as a finishing sauce.

Swaps & pairings

Garlic olive oil covers toast and sautéing without dairy. Roasted garlic mashed into plain butter is the homemade version.
