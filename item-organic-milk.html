Milk & Cream

Organic Milk

Milk produced under certified organic standards for feed, pasture, and herd care. It is sold at every fat level and is often ultra-pasteurized for a longer shelf life.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton. The printed date applies only while unopened.
Shelf lifeUnopened ultra-pasteurized cartons keep for weeks refrigerated; once opened, use within about 7 days.
Best usesDrinking, cereal, coffee, baking, and cooking.
SubstitutesRegular milk of the same fat level.
PairingsCereal, coffee, and tea.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

In the United States, organic milk must come from cows fed organic feed, given access to pasture, and not treated with antibiotics or growth hormones. Much of it is ultra-pasteurized, which is why organic cartons often carry later dates than regular milk.

Storing it

Once opened, organic milk spoils on the same schedule as regular milk. It freezes for up to 3 months; thaw in the fridge and shake.

Using it

Use organic milk exactly like conventional milk. Check whether your carton is ultra-pasteurized if you plan to make yogurt or cheese, since heating changes curd behavior.

Swaps & pairings

Regular whole or 2% milk swaps in one for one. Organic is a production standard, not a different ingredient in the recipe.
