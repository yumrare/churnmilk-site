Ice Cream & Frozen

Moose Tracks

A licensed American flavor: vanilla ice cream with peanut butter cups and a thick fudge ribbon. It is a premium, mix-in-heavy flavor and a consistent top seller.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Mix-ins go stale fastest; finish the pint promptly.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened. The cups stay distinct when kept deeply frozen.
Best usesSundaes, milkshakes, and eating straight from the pint.
SubstitutesPeanut butter cup ice creams from other makers, or vanilla with peanut butter cups folded in.
PairingsHot fudge, roasted peanuts, and chocolate sauce.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Moose Tracks was created in the 1980s by a Michigan ice cream company that licensed the name widely, which is why the same flavor, same name, and same moose illustration appear in freezers across brands. The formula is fixed by license: vanilla base, chocolate cups, fudge ribbon.

Storing it

Temper briefly so the fudge ribbon loosens without the cups turning greasy. Keep it airtight against odors.

Using it

Scoop moose tracks into a bowl with warm fudge sauce, or blend it into a thick shake with a little milk. Serve the fudge ribbon softened, not frozen hard.

Swaps & pairings

Any peanut butter cup flavor is near-identical in formula. Homemade vanilla with chopped cups and fudge sauce builds the same bowl.
