Yogurt & Cultured

Fruit Yogurt

Yogurt sweetened and flavored, most often with fruit preparations, vanilla, or honey. It is the sweet, ready-to-eat form of yogurt sold in single cups and tubes.

CategoryYogurt & Cultured
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep cups sealed and refrigerated. Stir fruit-on-the-bottom styles before eating.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; check the date on the cup.
Best usesSnacks, lunchboxes, breakfast, and smoothie bases.
SubstitutesPlain yogurt plus jam, honey, or fresh fruit.
PairingsGranola, fresh fruit, and lunchboxes.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Most flavored yogurt is fruit-on-the-bottom or blended; the blended style dominates single-serve cups. The fruit is usually a cooked, sweetened preparation folded in after culturing, which is why the yogurt underneath tastes the same across brands.

Storing it

Flavored yogurt freezes into acceptable frozen snacks for up to 2 months; eating quality after thawing is best in smoothies.

Using it

Eat fruit yogurt as a snack or breakfast, or fold it into smoothies. It also works in quick parfaits layered with granola and berries.

Swaps & pairings

Plain yogurt with a spoonful of jam is the same product assembled at the table, with the sweetness under your control.
