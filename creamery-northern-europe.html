Northern Europe

The butter heartland of Europe, from France to Denmark and Ireland, where higher-butterfat butter is the standard and pastry is built on it.

European-Style Butter

High-butterfat butter in the European style: 82 to 85 percent fat, less water than standard butter. The pastry butter, prized for laminated doughs and rich sauces.

Butter & Spreads · Northern Europe

Irish Butter

Butter made in Ireland from the milk of grass-fed cows, typically cultured and salted, with butterfat around 82 percent. It is deep yellow, dense, and rich.

Butter & Spreads · Northern Europe

Whey Butter

Butter churned from the cream that rises on whey left over from cheesemaking. It is slightly tangy, a little lower in fat than cream butter, and traditional in Britain.

Butter & Spreads · Northern Europe

Filmjölk

A Swedish cultured milk, mesophilic and mild, with a distinctive pourable, slightly ropy texture. It is eaten like a thin yogurt at breakfast.
