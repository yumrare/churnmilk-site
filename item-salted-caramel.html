Salted Caramel

An ice cream of caramel-flavored base with ribbons of salted caramel sauce. Sweet, buttery, and lightly salty, it is the modern parlor standard.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginFrance
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. The caramel ribbon freezes harder than the base; temper before serving.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened.
Best usesSundaes, milkshakes, blondies à la mode, and eating plain.
SubstitutesPlain caramel or dulce de leche ice cream for a sweeter, salt-free version.
PairingsChocolate, apples, pecans, and espresso.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Salted caramel's rise runs through Brittany, where salted-butter caramel (caramel au beurre salé) is traditional, to American menus in the 2000s. In ice cream the salt sharpens the caramel's toasted sugars, which cold otherwise mutes.

Storing it

Finish within about a month of opening for the cleanest caramel flavor. Keep it away from strong-smelling foods.

Using it

Let salted caramel temper so the ribbon softens with the base. Warm it slightly over a blondie or apple crisp for the classic pairing.

Swaps & pairings

Caramel or dulce de leche flavors swap directly, sweeter and gentler. A pinch of flaky salt over vanilla approximates the effect.
