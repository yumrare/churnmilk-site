Milk & Cream

Light Cream

Cream with about 18 to 30 percent butterfat, between half & half and whipping cream. It is a pouring cream for coffee and light sauces, and it does not whip.

CategoryMilk & Cream
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageRefrigerate at about 38°F in its sealed carton, in the back of the fridge.
Shelf lifeAbout 5 to 7 days after opening, and by the date on the carton.
Best usesCoffee, chowders, cream soups, sauces, and pouring over fruit.
SubstitutesHalf & half for a lighter result, or equal parts milk and heavy cream.
PairingsCoffee, berries, chowders, and scrambled eggs.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Light cream is the pour of diners and old cookbooks, richer than half & half and leaner than whipping cream. In coffee it gives body without coating the mouth, and in cooking it enriches sauces that heavy cream would make heavy.

Storing it

Light cream does not freeze well and may separate. Keep it sealed and use it within the week once opened.

Using it

Pour light cream over berries and into coffee, and use it in chowders where heavy cream would overwhelm. Do not try to whip it.

Swaps & pairings

Milk and heavy cream mixed in equal parts rebuild light cream closely. Half & half works in coffee and most sauces with less richness.
