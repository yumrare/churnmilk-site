Ice Cream & Frozen

Every frozen dairy in every form: ice cream, gelato, frozen custard, frozen yogurt, sherbet, soft serve, and novelties. Home of the Flavor Catalog, every ice cream flavor profiled like every other entry.

Vanilla

The classic ice cream flavor, made with real vanilla or vanilla extract. Vanilla is consistently among the best-selling ice cream flavors in the United States.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Chocolate

Ice cream flavored with cocoa or chocolate: one of the two oldest ice cream flavors and a permanent fixture in the top three sellers.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Strawberry

Sweet cream ice cream flavored with real strawberries or strawberry puree. One of the Neapolitan trio and a summertime staple.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Cookies & Cream

Vanilla ice cream loaded with crushed chocolate sandwich cookie pieces. An American invention that rose to fame in the early 1980s and never left the top flavors list.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Mint Chocolate Chip

Cool mint ice cream studded with chocolate pieces, often tinted pale green. Created in England in 1973 and now a global standard.

Ice Cream & Frozen · Torquay, England

Cookie Dough

Vanilla ice cream filled with chunks of chocolate chip cookie dough, usually with chocolate chips of its own. An American flavor popularized in 1984.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Butter Pecan

Vanilla or brown-sugar ice cream filled with butter-roasted pecans. A Southern American classic with a deep, toasted-nut flavor.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Rocky Road

Chocolate ice cream with marshmallow and nuts, born in Oakland, California in 1929. One of the first ice cream flavors with mix-ins.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Neapolitan

A tri-flavor ice cream sold in one carton, classically chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry in three distinct bands. It is a sliceable, ready-for-anyone dessert.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

French Vanilla

A premium vanilla ice cream made from a cooked egg-yolk custard base, rather than plain sweet cream. It is richer, denser, and yellower than standard vanilla.

Ice Cream & Frozen · France

Coffee

An ice cream flavored with coffee, ranging from lightly tan and sweet to dark and intense. It is a classic American parlor flavor and a base for coffee shakes.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Pistachio

An ice cream flavored with pistachio paste and dotted with nut pieces. Its flavor is rich, buttery, and unmistakably nutty, with a pale natural green.

Ice Cream & Frozen · Southern Italy

Salted Caramel

An ice cream of caramel-flavored base with ribbons of salted caramel sauce. Sweet, buttery, and lightly salty, it is the modern parlor standard.

Ice Cream & Frozen · France

Moose Tracks

A licensed American flavor: vanilla ice cream with peanut butter cups and a thick fudge ribbon. It is a premium, mix-in-heavy flavor and a consistent top seller.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Black Cherry

An almond-scented or vanilla base studded with dark, tart black cherries. It is pinkish-purple from the fruit, with a sweet-tart contrast.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Rum Raisin

An ice cream flavored with rum and studded with rum-soaked raisins. It is creamy and caramel-like, with a gentle alcoholic warmth.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Butter Brickle

A butter-flavored ice cream with pieces of butter brickle candy, a toffee of butter and brown sugar. It tastes of caramelized butter with a crunchy bite.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Spumoni

A molded Italian-style dessert of layered ice creams, classically chocolate, cherry, and pistachio, with candied fruit and nuts. It is served in slices, not scoops.

Ice Cream & Frozen · Southern Italy

Sherbet

A frozen dessert of fruit juice or purée with a little milk, between sorbet and ice cream in richness. It is icy, bright, and usually citrus or berry flavored.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Sorbet

A frozen dessert of sweetened fruit juice or purée and water, with no dairy. It is dense, intensely fruity, and icy-smooth when churned well.

Ice Cream & Frozen · France

Gelato

An Italian frozen dessert churned denser and served warmer than ice cream, with less air and usually less fat. Flavors taste more intense and the texture is silkier.

Ice Cream & Frozen · Southern Italy

Frozen Yogurt

A frozen dessert of yogurt and milk, sold in hard-packed pints and soft-serve shops. It ranges from tart and yogurt-forward to sweet and mild.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Soft Serve

A frozen dairy dessert served directly from a machine that freezes and aerates the mix at once. It is lighter, airier, and warmer than hard ice cream, sold in cones and cups.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States

Kulfi

An Indian frozen dessert of milk reduced by slow simmering until thick, sweetened and flavored (cardamom, saffron, pistachio), then frozen dense in molds.

Ice Cream & Frozen · India

Mochi Ice Cream

Bite-sized balls of ice cream wrapped in sweet rice dough (mochi). Each piece is one or two bites: chewy outside, frozen inside.

Ice Cream & Frozen · United States
