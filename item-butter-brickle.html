Butter Brickle

A butter-flavored ice cream with pieces of butter brickle candy, a toffee of butter and brown sugar. It tastes of caramelized butter with a crunchy bite.

CategoryIce Cream & Frozen
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageKeep sealed in the back of the freezer. Finish the pint promptly once opened for the candy's crunch.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 months in the freezer unopened; about 1 to 2 months once opened. The candy pieces stale first.
Best usesEating plain, sundaes, and over apple pie.
SubstitutesButter pecan for the same butter flavor with nuts instead of candy.
PairingsCaramel sauce, pecans, and apple pie.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Butter brickle was born in the 1920s as a butter brickle candy, and ice cream makers folded the candy in by the 1930s. The flavor later evolved into butter pecan, but butter brickle persists as its crunchier, more caramel ancestor.

Storing it

Temper briefly before scooping. Keep it airtight; the toffee pieces absorb odors.

Using it

Serve butter brickle as a sundae with warm caramel sauce, or à la mode with apple pie. Scoop it firm so the candy pieces stay crisp.

Swaps & pairings

Butter pecan is the direct modern descendant and the easiest swap. Vanilla with crushed butter toffee folded in rebuilds it at home.
