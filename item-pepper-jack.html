Pepper Jack

An American cheese made by flavoring Monterey Jack with spicy peppers, usually jalapeño, sometimes habanero and other chiles. It melts like Jack, with heat.

CategoryCheese
Maker / OriginUnited States
StorageWrap in wax paper, then loosely in plastic; store in the cheese drawer.
Shelf lifeAbout 2 to 3 weeks once opened. Keep wrapped; the pepper flecks do not change its keeping.
Best usesBurgers, nachos, quesadillas, grilled cheese, mac and cheese, and breakfast eggs.
SubstitutesMonterey Jack plus chopped jalapeño, or a spicy Havarti.
PairingsBurgers, nachos, lager, and grilled corn.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Pepper Jack began as Monterey Jack studded with peppers in the American Southwest. Makers tune the heat from gentle jalapeño warmth to habanero fire. Because the base cheese melts so well, the pepper version became a burger and nacho standard.

Storing it

Keep sealed to protect other foods from its spice. Pepper Jack freezes acceptably when shredded, up to 2 months.

Using it

Melt Pepper Jack anywhere Monterey Jack goes when the dish wants heat: burgers, nachos, and mac and cheese. Grate it for even melting.

Swaps & pairings

Plain Monterey Jack with fresh or pickled jalapeño stirred in is the exact formula. Spiced Havarti gives a softer, milder version.
