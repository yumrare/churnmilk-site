Yogurt & Cultured

Balkan Yogurt

A set-style yogurt fermented undisturbed in its container, giving a firm, clean-cut gel and a pronounced tartness. It is made with Balkan cultures, typically without thickeners.

CategoryYogurt & Cultured
Maker / OriginBalkans
StorageKeep sealed in its tub in the fridge. Once spooned into, the gel weeps whey; pour it off or stir it in.
Shelf lifeAbout 1 to 3 weeks in the fridge; check the date. The set breaks once stirred, which is normal.
Best usesEating plain, with honey and fruit, in dressings, and in Balkan dishes such as tarator.
SubstitutesPlain yogurt, or Greek yogurt for a thicker, less tart result.
PairingsHoney, fruit, granola, and cucumber salads.
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Balkan yogurt's set is its signature: the culture works undisturbed in the container, which is why opening a tub shows a clean, glossy surface. The style is associated with Bulgarian and Balkan traditions of thick, tart yogurt making.

Storing it

Balkan yogurt freezes for up to 2 months for cooking and smoothies; the set texture does not survive thawing.

Using it

Eat Balkan yogurt un stirred, spooning through the set, or whisk it into cucumber soup (tarator) and dressings.

Swaps & pairings

Any plain set yogurt swaps directly. Greek yogurt is thicker and milder; plain stirred yogurt is looser.
